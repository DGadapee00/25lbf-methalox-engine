The 25 lbf methalox test stand, before it exists

An operable model of the engine's feed system and chamber: bottles, regulators, valves, injectors, the chamber and the purge. Open valves, fail a regulator, light the chamber, rehearse a sequence with aborts, and fit the model to test data.

It is a tool for being proven wrong by hardware as early as possible. Most component values are placeholders and nothing is calibrated yet; the orange uncalibrated badge means exactly that. It never replaces a test.

  • Guided path: thirteen short lessons, from one bottle emptying to a hot fire, its aborts, the predict-test-fit loop and the open choke-margin question. The next thing to press is marked in yellow.
  • On your own: hover anything on the schematic for what it is and its live state; answer the Predict first cards; open the Glossary for the terms.
